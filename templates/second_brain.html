Start Here

A practical daily workflow for this workspace.

Morning

  1. Set Today's Focus on the dashboard.
  2. Check Habits and mark what you will do.
  3. Open Daily Planner — energy, Top 3, schedule notes.
  4. Scan Tasks due today; move 1–3 into Top 3.
🏠 Dashboard focus ✓ Habits 📅 Daily Planner ☑ Tasks

During the day

  1. Use Quick Capture for anything that pops up — do not organize mid-flow.
  2. Work tasks by priority and context (Deep Work vs Quick Win).
  3. Park content ideas on the Content board without polishing.
⚡ Quick Capture ☑ Tasks 🎬 Content Ideas

Evening

  1. Process Inbox: convert to Task / Note or mark processed.
  2. Log Wins in Daily Planner; update Habit checkboxes.
  3. Move finished tasks to Done; nudge project status if needed.
⚡ Process Inbox 📅 Log wins 📁 Projects

Weekly

Open Weekly Review: wins, lessons, next-week plan, rating. Adjust Goals progress and archive completed projects.
